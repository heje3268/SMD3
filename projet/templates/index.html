Why this page ?

On April 24, 2022, Emmanuel Macron and Marine Le Pen faced off in the presidential election. Some areas of France voted overwhelmingly for one candidate, while others were more divided.. This page aims to compare these different voting patterns by region and department.

    How to read these numbers ?

    Abstention
    Percentage of registered voters who did not vote.
    Blank and invalid votes
    Percentage of voters whose ballots do not name any candidate. These ballots are not counted as votes cast.
    Votes received by the candidates
    Percentage of votes cast. In the second round, there are only two candidates, so their combined total is always 100%.
    Regions and All of France
    These figures are recalculated by adding up the votes from all the departments in question. This is not the average of the percentages, because a highly populated department carries more weight than a small one.
